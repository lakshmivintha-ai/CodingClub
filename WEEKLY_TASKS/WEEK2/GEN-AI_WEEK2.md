##### **DEEP LEARNING :** 

###### Deep Learning is a part of Machine Learning that uses neural networks with many layers to learn patterns from large amounts of data 

* ###### In AI there is Machine Learning ,In Machine Learning there is Deep Learning 

##### **Token :** 

###### A Token is a small piece of text that an AI language model processes.

* ###### A Token can be a whole word,part of a word , punctuation 
* ###### Tokens are matter because language models dont process your entire sentence as one giant object 

##### **AI Agents :** 

###### An AI agent is a system that can use an AI model to work toward a goal by deciding what steps to takeand , when given the appropriate tools/permissions , taking actions . 

##### **There are 4 types of learning used in AI/ML :** 

1. ###### Supervised Learning 

###### 2\. Unsupervised Learning 

###### 3\. Semi - Supervised Learning 

###### 4.Reinforcement Learning 

##### **SUPERVISED LEARNING :** 

###### In supervised learning ,the AI learns from data where the correct answer is already given .

###### Common Uses : Spam detection , Disease classification , Image classification , House - price , ...

##### **UNSUPERVISED LEARNING :** 

###### In unsupervised learning , the AI receives data without correct answers/labels.

###### Common Uses : Customer segmentation , Finding groups in data , Anomaly detection , Pattern discovery 

##### **SEMI-SUPERVISED LEARNING :** 

###### This is a combination of supervised and unsupervised learning 

* ###### The AI uses the labelled examples to learn and also makes use of the large amount of unlabelled data .

##### **REINFORCEMENT LEARNIG :** 

###### The AI learns by interacting with an environment and receiving rewards or penalties.
